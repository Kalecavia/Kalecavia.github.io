# World Pulse image-to-video prompts

## Tool settings

- Tool: MagicLight
- Model: Seedance 2.0 standard
- Resolution: 4K
- Duration: 5 seconds
- Audio: none
- Goal: restrained documentary motion that preserves the source photograph

## Food and water — submitted Pro render

> Preserve the exact hyperrealistic irrigation valley photograph, all terrain
> geometry, waterways, crops, clouds and natural dawn lighting. Create a premium
> documentary 5-second drone shot with an extremely slow forward glide, subtle
> parallax across the fields, gently moving water reflections, faint morning
> mist and minimal vegetation movement. Photographic optics, physically
> plausible motion, stable exposure, detailed 4K look. No new buildings, no
> fantasy elements, no morphing, no warping, no flicker, no text, no logo,
> silent, smooth near-seamless loop.

## Prompt guardrails

- Preserve geometry and composition.
- Motion must stay slow enough for a looping editorial background.
- Avoid cinematic exaggeration, fantasy VFX and synthetic particles.
- No text, logos, watermarks, morphing, flicker or exposure pumping.
- Prefer physical camera motion, atmospheric drift and small practical motion.
