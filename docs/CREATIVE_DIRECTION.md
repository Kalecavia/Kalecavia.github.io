# Creative direction — The Living World Interface

## Narrative

The visitor enters at orbital scale and moves through the planet's invisible
systems: time, people, resources, pressure and human outcomes. Scroll changes
the camera, density, color temperature and spatial behavior of one continuous
world machine.

## Visual language

- **Deep space:** near-black blue instead of generic black.
- **Signal cyan:** observation, movement and neutral data.
- **Living green:** food, water, health and renewal.
- **Pressure coral:** emissions, scarcity and urgent trade-offs.
- **Paper white:** editorial text and moments of calm.
- **Warm sand:** human-scale context and historical notes.

The page combines monumental display typography, restrained editorial copy,
technical monospaced labels and large areas of breathing room. Glow is a
signal, not decoration.

## Motion grammar

- Slow orbital movement establishes scale.
- Pulses and particle transfers communicate continuous systems.
- Camera travel is tied to section progress, never to wheel velocity alone.
- Content enters with short fades and vertical shifts.
- Reduced motion replaces continuous travel with stable compositions and
  instant state changes.

## 3D direction

One persistent scene transforms instead of loading seven unrelated scenes:

- triangulated data globe;
- atmosphere shells and orbital arcs;
- population clusters;
- resource reservoirs and transfer lanes;
- pressure halo with warmer spectrum;
- human-scale constellation;
- system overview with all layers aligned.

The scene is procedural, lightweight and art-directed in code. Essential
numbers and explanations remain semantic HTML outside the canvas.

## Anti-patterns

- No neon cyberpunk dashboard.
- No fake satellite imagery or invented geography.
- No autoplay sound.
- No constant camera shake or scroll hijacking.
- No unsupported claim that modeled counters are live measurements.
