# Project brief — World Pulse

## Product promise

World Pulse turns public world statistics into an explorable system. The core
idea is simple: global data are not isolated counters; they are the pulse of
one connected planet.

## Audience

- Curious general readers who want context before detail.
- Students, educators and journalists seeking source-aware summaries.
- Design-conscious visitors who expect a memorable interactive experience.

## Primary user flows

1. Understand the premise within the opening viewport.
2. Scroll from planetary to human scale through seven narrative chapters.
3. Open a theme page for clearer statistics and interpretation.
4. Inspect the source, date, update cadence and methodological caveat.
5. Use the full experience with keyboard, reduced motion or without WebGL.

## Scope

### P0

- Premium multi-route site with working navigation.
- Seven-chapter homepage with one responsive realtime Three.js scene.
- Population, resources, energy/emissions and health theme pages.
- Typed, source-aware static data layer.
- Sources/methodology and about pages.
- Mobile, reduced-motion and no-WebGL experiences.
- Lint, typecheck, build and route smoke tests.

### P1

- A single purpose-built social preview image.
- Optional short generated transition film when it materially improves a
  chapter and stays within the performance budget.
- Deeper browser-based responsive and accessibility QA.

### P2

- Automated source ingestion and scheduled snapshots.
- Country explorer and comparison tools.
- Personal data playlists, saved views or accounts.
- Audio narration and multilingual content.

## Success criteria

The site should feel cinematic without hiding information, credible without
becoming academic, and technically ambitious without turning every section
into a GPU effect.
